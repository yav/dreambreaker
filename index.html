Dreambreaker Simulator

Rules: random initial serve; if serving side loses a rally, serve switches. At 20+ points, a team can score only while serving. To win: 21+ points and ahead by 2. Each matchup plays up to 4 rallies.

Sliders: chance to win a rally. Drag matchups to rearrange.

Averages are in the form (P10,avg,P90), where PX means that X% of the data has a value less than the given number.
